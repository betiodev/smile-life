# Prompts for the cards that might be refused

Written 2026-11-11 by Claude Fable. Only **new extension cards** whose subject could trip ChatGPT's image rules: violence, weapons, sex, drugs, death, disasters with victims, politics, real people, brands, copyrighted characters. Cards not listed here are safe to describe in your own words.

Each entry gives a prompt that keeps the joke while showing a symbol, a metaphor or the aftermath instead of the thing itself, and a fallback that is tamer still.

**Honest warning:** these are written to stay clearly inside the rules, but nobody can promise what an image tool accepts on a given day. If both prompts are refused, describe only the object and leave out the card's name.

Card names come from the leaflets. A few cards of Trash, Politique and Luxe are not named there; once your tables are filled, anything risky among them can be handled the same way.

## How to use

1. Start a ChatGPT conversation and paste the **style block** below as your first message, with two or three existing illustrations attached.
2. Then paste one card prompt per message.
3. Templates: where a prompt starts with **[SIGN]**, **[OBJECT]**, **[CHARACTER]** or **[SCENE]**, paste the matching template line first, then the rest.

### Style block (paste once at the start)

```
I am illustrating a private, humorous card game for adults. Please keep one consistent style for every image in this conversation: bright glossy cartoon sticker style, thick dark outlines, saturated colours, soft cel shading with a few shiny highlights, rounded friendly shapes. Square image. Transparent or plain white background. One clear subject, centred, with some empty space around it. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos anywhere in the image. Nothing realistic, gory or frightening: everything is cute, silly and family-cartoon in tone. No real people and no existing characters.
```

### Templates

```
[SIGN] A red triangular warning road sign on a short metal post, cartoon sticker style. Inside the sign, on a cream background:
```
```
[OBJECT] A single cartoon object, like a chunky toy or a sticker, alone on a white background, not held by anyone:
```
```
[CHARACTER] One cartoon character, full body, friendly caricature, alone on a white background, with only the props they hold or touch:
```
```
[SCENE] A small symbolic cartoon scene with no people at all, on a white background:
```

For Fantastique maléfices use [SIGN] but ask for a **purple** sign.
